## Knowledge Article Standard

Each article must contain:
- Definition
- Why it matters
- When to use
- When not to use
- Architecture
- Inputs and outputs
- Risks and controls
- Examples
- Evaluation criteria
- Sources
- Owner
- Review date
- Version

## Source Hierarchy

1. Internal policy and approved system records
2. Primary vendor documentation
3. Standards bodies and peer-reviewed research
4. Verified technical references
5. Model-generated synthesis marked as such

## Freshness Rules

- APIs, models, prices, release channels, hardware requirements, and laws require scheduled review.
- Stable conceptual lessons may use annual review.
- Deprecated or preview features must be tagged visibly.
