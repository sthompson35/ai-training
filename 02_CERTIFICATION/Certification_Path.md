# AI Training Academy — Certification Path

## Certification Framework

Certifications validate demonstrated capability, not attendance. Every award requires knowledge, practical execution, evidence, and ethical operating behavior.

## Pathway

### Tier 1 — AI Foundations Associate
Required levels: 00–02  
Exam: 60 questions  
Practical: Design and test a structured prompt  
Passing score: 80%

### Tier 2 — Client and Hybrid AI Practitioner
Required levels: 03–07  
Practical: Build an AI content feature with on-device handling, server fallback, offline messaging, and normalized results.

### Tier 3 — Knowledge and Model Adaptation Engineer
Required levels: 08–09  
Practical: Build a grounded retrieval workflow and justify prompting, RAG, or fine-tuning.

### Tier 4 — Certified AI Agent Developer
Required levels: 10–12  
Practical: Deliver a tool-using agent with bounded permissions, structured handoffs, approvals, and audit history.

### Tier 5 — AI Platform Engineer
Required levels: 13–15  
Practical: Deploy, monitor, secure, evaluate, and roll back an AI service.

### Tier 6 — Enterprise AI Architect
Required levels: 16–17  
Board review: Defend business value, architecture, governance, resiliency, economics, and capstone evidence.

## Recertification

- Annual policy and security update
- Biennial practical revalidation
- Immediate remediation following a critical production incident caused by noncompliance
- Certification status: Active, Conditional, Suspended, or Expired

## Evidence Requirements

- Architecture diagram
- Threat model
- Test plan and evaluation results
- Cost and capacity estimate
- Data classification record
- Approval and escalation matrix
- Monitoring dashboard definition
- Rollback procedure
- Lessons learned
