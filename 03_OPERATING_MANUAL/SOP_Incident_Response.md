# SOP: AI Incident Response

1. Detect and classify severity.
2. Stop harmful execution; disable feature flag or revoke tool access.
3. Preserve prompts, outputs, logs, model versions, and affected records.
4. Identify user, data, financial, legal, and operational impact.
5. Notify the incident owner and required stakeholders.
6. Roll back to a verified version or deterministic fallback.
7. Correct the defect and expand regression tests.
8. Conduct root-cause analysis.
9. Document corrective and preventive actions.
10. Reauthorize before restoring full service.
